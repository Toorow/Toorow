# Bullet forms

A ratified page states its criteria under a heading, and the heading is allowed
to carry a paragraph of its own before the list starts.

## Incomplete if

The list below is the contract of this page.

- a dashed bullet, stated on one line
- a dashed bullet whose sentence
  continues on the following line
1. a numbered bullet, because two ratified pages number theirs
2. a numbered bullet that ends on a semicolon;

- a bullet separated from the list above by a blank line

    an indented block that belongs to no bullet, because the blank line above
    closed the one before it

## Something else entirely

- a bullet under a heading that opens no list, and therefore no criterion
