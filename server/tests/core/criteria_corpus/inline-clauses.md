# Clauses written outside a list

*Incomplete if*: a clause stated at column zero, ahead of the page's own list.

## Incomplete if

- the only bullet of the established list

## Amendments

Add to **Incomplete if**:

- a bullet under the end-of-line opener, which belongs to the established list

Add to **Incomplete if**: a clause carried on the opener's own line, which does not.

**Incomplete if** (adds to the list above):

- a bullet under an inline opener, which is appended and never listed

A sentence that ends here. *Incomplete if*: a clause stated mid-paragraph.

1. A numbered step of a procedure.

    *Incomplete if*: a clause indented under a numbered step, after a blank line.

2. Another numbered step, whose sentence runs on.
    *Incomplete if*: a continuation of the sentence above, and not a clause.
