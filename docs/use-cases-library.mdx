When an agent calls a **toorow** tool, the host receives two channels at once: a
short text summary the model can read, and a structured payload a host can draw.
The second one is what turns an answer into something you can look at.

---

## The two channels

```
  TEXT CHANNEL  (≤ 30 lines)          STRUCTURED CHANNEL (structuredContent)
  ────────────────────────────        ──────────────────────────────────────
  What the model reads and can        Metric rows, provenance, freshness,
  quote in its answer.                definitions and quality findings.
                                      What the host draws.
```

A summary that replaces the structured payload turns a chart into a paragraph —
see [Agent Rules](/agent-rules), rule 4.

---

## The visual surfaces that exist

| Surface | What it is | How an agent reaches it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cards** | Ten registered card templates, each answering one question from governed inputs: `kpi`, `keywords`, `conversions`, `usertypes`, `journey`, `attribution`, `videos`, `dedup`, `mediaplan_pacing`, `connectors`. | `list_card_templates` to choose, then `get_card` |
| **MCP App widgets** | Single-file React widgets under `ui/widgets/`, rendered inside the host. Three ship today. | Returned as the app channel of the tool that owns them |
| **Renders and Dossiers** | A frozen figure, or a document composing several — addressable, versioned, shareable. | `render_analyze_result`, then `compose_dossier` — see [Renders, Dossiers and Sharing](/renders-and-sharing) |

## Use cases

### 1. The daily read
- **Intent** — *"How did last week go?"*
- **Tools** — `get_daily_report(project_id)` for the numbers, then
  `preview_daily_insight(project_id, payload)` for the narrative that goes with
  them. `get_daily_insight_readiness` first says whether there is anything worth
  saying.
- **What the reader gets** — a `kpi` card, and every figure carries the pull that
  produced it and how fresh it is.

### 2. Comparing channels
- **Intent** — *"Compare spend efficiency across Meta, TikTok and GA4."*
- **Tools** — `get_daily_report(project_id, connectors=["meta-ads", "tiktok-ads", "google-analytics"])`,
  or a governed question through `explore_analyze_query`.
- **The trap it avoids** — CTR, CPA and ROAS are not additive. The semantic ratio
  views recompute them at the grain you asked for, so a cross-channel comparison
  is not a sum of ratios. See [Agent Rules](/agent-rules), rule 2.

### 3. Explaining a drop
- **Intent** — *"Why did conversions fall on 14 July?"*
- **Tools** — `get_daily_report` for the shape of the dip, `search_context` and
  `get_context_hub` for what the business registered around that date.
- **The rule that holds** — context events are offered as **candidate context**,
  never as causes. toorow records that a deployment and a drop share a date; it
  does not know that one produced the other.

### 4. Auditing what is connected
- **Intent** — *"Show me every datastream and its mapping status."*
- **Tools** — `flows_list(project_id)`, then `flows_get(project_id, kind, id)` for
  one definition. `get_data_quality_report` says which of the ten monitors is
  unhappy and where.
- **What the reader gets** — the `connectors` card, and a repair named for each
  finding rather than a status code.

### 5. Keeping the analysis
- **Intent** — *"Turn this into something I can send."*
- **Tools** — `render_analyze_result` freezes each figure, `compose_dossier`
  composes them with the narrative into an immutable version, and a Share hands
  it out — requested by one person, confirmed by a second.
- **What the reader gets** — a page that draws through the same runtime as the
  console, with each figure's provenance beside it. See
  [Renders, Dossiers and Sharing](/renders-and-sharing).
